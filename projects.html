My Projects

Automated Clothesline Machine Using Arduino

This was a school research project focused on creating an automated clothesline machine that can help households protect clothes from rain.

My Role: I worked with my group members in developing and documenting the project.

Tools Used: Arduino UNO, rain sensor, DC motors, relay module, limit switches, and Arduino IDE.

RJ45 Crossover and Straight-Through Cable Crimping

This was a hands-on activity I completed during Senior High School. I learned how to create RJ45 crossover and straight-through cables by arranging the wires correctly and crimping them using an RJ45 connector.

My Role: I personally worked on preparing, arranging, and crimping the network cables.

Tools Used: RJ45 connectors, UTP cable, RJ45 crimping tool, and cable tester.
